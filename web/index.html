What the labels mean

Source-reported
The retailer published this price. We have not completed a checkout or confirmed stock.
Checked
When DealRadar last successfully read that price. Old observations stop counting as current.
Was price
Shown only when the retailer's own original price is higher than the sale price. Otherwise it is removed.
Unit price
Normalized to 100 g, 100 ml, roll, or unit when the package size is explicit.
